Flux is an open-source music player for Android that streams the YouTube Music catalog without ads, with a fluid interface that reacts to the music.

Highlights:
* Listen Together — create a room and play the same song, in sync, with your friends.
* Find songs — identify the music playing around you and open it in Flux with one tap.
* Themes — apply ready-made themes or import a .json theme file, no reinstall needed.
* Living player — the play button flows and pulses with the beat, the cover gets an aura that reacts to bass, mids and treble, and the mini player morphs into the full player.
* Album colors — the theme follows the song that is playing, with automatic contrast so text stays readable.
* Liquid glass — frosted-glass bars and buttons.
* Synced lyrics — word-by-word lyrics from several providers, with optional translation.
* Offline — download songs, albums and playlists.
* Smart mixes — personal mixes built on your device from what you listen to most.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking: your library and settings stay on your device.

Flux is a fork of Echo Music and is licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0.
